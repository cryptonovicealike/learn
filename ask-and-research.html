Privacy notice

The information you provide (your optional name, required email, request type, and the relevant question or research details) is collected to receive, assess, manage, and respond to your request. Your email is required; without it, we cannot respond. Your name is optional.

Formspree processes and stores submissions as a service provider. Authorized Crypto Novice Alike administrators can access requests. Formspree may process data according to its own terms and privacy practices. Request information is not used for newsletter subscriptions or marketing emails.

You may contact the responsible party to ask about access, correction, deletion, or objection to processing, subject to applicable legal requirements. You may also lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za.

Do not include passwords, wallet seed phrases, private keys, or other sensitive credentials.

Launch prerequisite: before accepting submissions, replace this notice's configuration details with the verified responsible-party name and contact details, Information Officer contact, Formspree processing/retention details, and whether or how information may be transferred outside South Africa. Also state any other recipients and retention period or criteria. These details depend on the responsible party's identity and Formspree account configuration and have not been supplied for this preview.
