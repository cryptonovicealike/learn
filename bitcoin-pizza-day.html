So Let’s Talk About Bitcoin Pizza Day 🍕

What is Bitcoin Pizza Day?

Bitcoin Pizza Day is an annual celebration in the crypto community that takes place on May 22. It commemorates one of the most famous transactions in Bitcoin history.

On May 22, 2010, programmer Laszlo Hanyecz successfully used Bitcoin to buy two pizzas. He traded 10,000 BTC for the two pizzas, in what became the first widely recognized real-world purchase of physical goods using Bitcoin.

At the time, Bitcoin was still a very young project and had almost no established market value compared with today. The 10,000 BTC were worth roughly $40–$41 at the time of the transaction.

The transaction was arranged through the BitcoinTalk forum. Hanyecz had posted an offer to pay 10,000 BTC to anyone who would get him two large pizzas. A fellow BitcoinTalk user known as “jercos,” Jeremy Sturdivant, accepted the offer and arranged for the pizzas to be delivered.

This simple pizza purchase became an important moment in Bitcoin's history because it demonstrated something that had previously been mostly theoretical: Bitcoin could actually be exchanged for real-world goods.

And yes, those two pizzas have become one of the most famous examples of how dramatically Bitcoin's value has changed. The 10,000 BTC that were worth around $41 in 2010 would be worth hundreds of millions of dollars at modern Bitcoin prices. The exact value changes constantly with the Bitcoin market price.

The significance of Bitcoin Pizza Day

Real-world use

The pizza transaction showed that Bitcoin could move beyond being an idea discussed online and actually be used to purchase something in the real world.

It became an early example of Bitcoin functioning as a medium of exchange, which is one of the fundamental purposes of money.

An early price benchmark

The transaction also gives us an interesting look at Bitcoin's extremely early value.

The 10,000 BTC were worth approximately $40–$41 at the time, meaning the transaction implied a Bitcoin price of roughly $0.004 per BTC.

That makes the story even more remarkable when you compare Bitcoin's early value with its much higher valuations in later years.

A milestone for Bitcoin adoption

The pizza purchase did not single-handedly turn Bitcoin into a global financial asset, but it became an important historical milestone in Bitcoin's development.

It demonstrated that people could actually exchange Bitcoin for goods and services, helping provide an early example of how cryptocurrency could function outside of a purely theoretical or experimental environment.

Community bonding

Bitcoin Pizza Day has also become a way for the cryptocurrency community to celebrate Bitcoin's early history.

Every May 22, crypto enthusiasts remember the famous transaction and reflect on how far Bitcoin has come since 2010.

How much would the 10,000 BTC be worth today?

This is probably the most interesting part of the story.

Laszlo Hanyecz spent 10,000 BTC on two pizzas in 2010. If he had somehow kept those Bitcoin instead, their value today would be enormous.

However, there is an important point to remember: the value isn't fixed. Bitcoin's price changes constantly, so the value of 10,000 BTC changes with it.

This is why Bitcoin Pizza Day often inspires what people sometimes call the “Bitcoin Pizza Index” — a fun way of calculating what those 10,000 BTC would be worth at different Bitcoin prices.

The comparison is fascinating, but we should also remember that Laszlo's transaction helped demonstrate that Bitcoin could actually be used as money. Without people being willing to spend Bitcoin in its early days, it would have been much harder to demonstrate its usefulness as a medium of exchange.

Final thoughts

Bitcoin Pizza Day is a reminder of just how different the cryptocurrency world looked in 2010.

What started as one programmer exchanging 10,000 BTC for two pizzas became one of the most famous stories in cryptocurrency history.

Today, Bitcoin Pizza Day is celebrated every May 22 by crypto enthusiasts and serves as a reminder of Bitcoin's journey from a small experimental digital currency into a globally recognized financial asset.
