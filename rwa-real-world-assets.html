RWA: Real-World Assets

RWA, or Real-World Assets, is basically the idea of bringing real-world assets such as real estate, government bonds, commodities, art, credit and collectibles onto the blockchain.

The goal is to represent these assets digitally through blockchain-based tokens, making ownership, transfer and access more efficient and potentially more transparent. In some cases, tokenization can also allow an asset to be divided into smaller portions, creating the possibility of fractional ownership. However, tokenization does not automatically make an asset safe, liquid or accessible to everyone. The legal structure, regulations, custody arrangements and rights attached to the token still matter.

Epic Chain

Epic Chain (EPIC) is a blockchain project that evolved from Ethernity Chain, a platform originally known for authenticated digital collectibles and celebrity-related NFTs. In 2025, Ethernity Chain rebranded as Epic Chain following a community vote, with the project expanding its vision toward blockchain infrastructure for entertainment and real-world assets.

Epic Chain's earlier post-rebrand vision included an ecosystem combining entertainment, NFTs, decentralized applications and RWA tokenization. Its RWA ambitions included areas such as collectibles and other forms of real-world value. Fanable, for example, was positioned as a consumer-facing platform for bringing physical and collectible assets into Web3.
